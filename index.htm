The Orthova System

Built for the
Modern Practice.

Orthova is a clear aligner system designed around the clinician. Every element — from material selection to digital planning tools — is engineered to give you predictable outcomes, streamlined chair time, and the confidence to case-plan with precision. No compromises on quality. No complexity in workflow.

01

Material Integrity

Medical-grade, bio-compatible thermoform polymers selected for optical clarity, consistent force delivery, and patient comfort throughout the treatment arc.

02

Each aligner is digitally planned and manufactured to exacting tolerances — gingival trim lines, pressure zones, and staging sequences are calibrated for controlled, predictable movement.

Clinical Workflow

From Scan to Delivery

1

Case Submission

Submit intraoral scans (STL/PLY) or impressions through our secure portal. Include your prescription, treatment objectives, and any specific staging preferences.

2

Digital Setup & Approval

Our technicians build a full 3D treatment plan. You review the staging sequence, request modifications, and approve the final setup before any fabrication begins.

3

Precision Fabrication

Aligners are thermoformed from medical-grade polymer, trimmed to your specified gingival line, polished, and quality-checked at each stage of production.

4

Delivery & Ongoing Support

Full aligner sets ship directly to your clinic with labelled staging. Mid-course corrections, refinements, and retention appliances are handled with the same precision.
